-- Verdict ---------------------------------------------------------------

   A  197657  operon start      core_6mer   6-mer TAAAAT (0 mm), 9-mer GTGTAAAAT (1 mm)
   B  197721  depth jump       no_minus10   6-mer TATTAT (1 mm), 9-mer TGTTATTAT (1 mm)

   The operon start carries a perfect TANAAT hexamer (TAAAAT) at register 0,
   i.e. canonical -10 spacing; the depth-jump coordinate carries no -10 box at any
   register within +/-2 bp, and is one of the 6-mer-negative positions in its own
   neighbourhood. The 6.8-fold coverage step at 197721 therefore marks a
   post-transcriptional boundary inside a transcript initiated at 197657, not a
   second promoter. (What kind of boundary -- endonucleolytic cut vs 5'->3' trimming
   stall -- is NOT settled by these data; see the rejected RBS argument in the
   docstring.)

   Caveat: A reaches the core_6mer tier, not strong_9mer -- the 9-mer extension misses
   by 1 (GTGTAAAAT). That is the majority situation among real Syn1 operons
   (87/127 match the 6-mer, only 52/127 the 9-mer), so it does not weaken the call.

-- Verdict (0178) --------------------------------------------------------

   234511  0178 5' end     no_minus10   6-mer TAATAT (1 mm), 9-mer AATTAATAT (2 mm)

   The consensus is not matched: TAATAT is 1 mismatch from TANAAT at every register
   within +/-2 bp. Treat this as a scanner non-assignment, NOT as evidence that the
   5' end is not a promoter -- a single deviation from consensus is ordinary in real
   bacterial -10 elements, and 40/127 (31%) of the canonical Syn1 operons fall in the
   same bin. The scan is simply uninformative here, in either direction.

   What does stand on its own: the 0178 5' end is sharp, dominant (93% of reads) and
   intergenic. The affirmative -10 argument is available for lap (Part 1); it is not
   claimed for 0178.
