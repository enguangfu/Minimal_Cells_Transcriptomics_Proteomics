% Long-read transcriptomics revealed extensive transcript isoform diversity across the Syn1 genome. After clustering FLNC reads based on transcript boundaries, 82,806 transcript isoforms were identified. Classification of isoforms relative to genome annotations revealed a subset of 816 abnormal transcripts, including antisense transcripts overlapping annotated genes, transcripts originating from intergenic regions, and transcripts spanning gene boundaries. These transcripts represent potential sources of non-canonical translation products.

% ORF prediction and initiation scoring identified 95 high-confidence candidate ORFs encoded by abnormal transcripts. These predicted proteins had a median length of 27 amino acids and were supported by a median of 42 PacBio reads, indicating that they originate from reproducible transcriptional events. In silico trypsin digestion showed that 94 of the 95 candidates contained at least one peptide unique relative to the canonical Syn1 proteome, demonstrating that the vast majority of predicted translation products are experimentally distinguishable by bottom-up proteomics. These sequences were compiled into a custom proteogenomics database to enable validation through re-analysis of existing mass spectrometry datasets.
