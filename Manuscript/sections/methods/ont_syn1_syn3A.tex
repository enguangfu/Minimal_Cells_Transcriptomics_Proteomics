\subsubsection{ONT RNA Sample Preparation}

Direct-RNA samples for~\jsyn~and~\jsyna~were prepared by a shared protocol adapted from~\cite{grunberger_next_2019}.
One mL aliquots of actively growing cells were placed in 25 mL of SP4 + 17\% KnockOut serum replacement in 50 mL conical tubes and incubated overnight at 37\textdegree C.
The cells were harvested in mid-late log phase, indicated by a red-orange color of the phenol red pH indicator, when the media pH reached 7.0;~\syn~cultures reached this stage in 16 hours and~\syna~cultures in 22 hours.
Growth was arrested by placing the tubes on wet ice for 10 minutes.
Keeping all tubes on ice, 1.4 mL of bulk culture was distributed into fifteen 1.8 mL microfuge tubes per culture and centrifuged at 4\textdegree C for five minutes at 10,000$\times$g to pellet the cells.
The supernatants from ten tubes were aspirated, and the pellets were progressively resuspended and reconcentrated into five tubes of concentrated cell suspension.
These were centrifuged again (five minutes, 4\textdegree C, 10,000$\times$g), the supernatants aspirated completely, and the pellets snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80\textdegree C.

Cellular RNA was extracted using a phenol-chloroform protocol with the ThermoFisher TRIzol\textsuperscript{TM} reagent.
1 mL of 4\textdegree C TRIzol\textsuperscript{TM} was added to each frozen pellet and pipetted to break up the pellet, followed by a 5-minute room-temperature incubation.
200 $\mu$L of chloroform was added, the tubes were shaken for 30 seconds, incubated for 3 minutes, and centrifuged at 12,000$\times$g for 15 minutes at 4\textdegree C.
The upper aqueous phase was transferred, avoiding the interphase, into tubes containing 500 $\mu$L isopropanol, incubated on ice for 10 minutes, and centrifuged at 12,000$\times$g for 10 minutes at 4\textdegree C.
The supernatant was discarded, the pellet air-dried for 15 minutes, and the RNA dissolved in 20 $\mu$L of RNase-free water containing 0.1 mM EDTA.
RNA concentration was assessed with a Qubit RNA HS fluorometer assay and an Agilent TapeStation 2200.

Ribosomal RNA (rRNA) was then depleted.
The~\syna~sample and the second~\syn~sample were depleted with the NEBNext\textregistered{} rRNA Depletion Kit for bacteria, whereas the first~\syn~sample was depleted with the Invitrogen RiboMinus\textsuperscript{TM} Bacteria 2.0 Transcriptome Isolation Kit.
For the NEBNext\textregistered{} protocol, 1 $\mu$g of RNA was brought up to 11 $\mu$L with RNase-free water, combined with 2 $\mu$L of NEBNext\textregistered{} Bacterial rRNA Depletion Solution and 2 $\mu$L of NEBNext\textregistered{} Probe Hybridization Buffer, heated to 95\textdegree C for 2 minutes, and cooled to 22\textdegree C at 0.1\textdegree C/sec.
This was followed immediately by RNase H digestion (2 $\mu$L of RNase H Reaction Buffer and 2 $\mu$L of NEBNext Thermostable RNase H, total 20 $\mu$L, 50\textdegree C for 30 minutes) and an Agencourt RNAClean XP bead cleanup (Beckman Coulter\texttrademark, cat \# A63987).

Depleted RNA was 3$'$ poly(A) tailed with a premade solution of 2 $\mu$L of 10X~\eco~Poly(A) Polymerase Reaction Buffer, 2 $\mu$L of ATP, and 1 $\mu$L of~\eco~Poly(A) Polymerase (New England Biolabs), incubated at 37\textdegree C for 30 minutes, and stopped with EDTA (10 mM final).
After a further bead cleanup and quality control, RNA was normalized to 9 $\mu$L containing 140 ng for~\syn~and 50 ng for~\syna.

\subsubsection{ONT RNA Sample Quality Control Analysis}

Both a Qubit RNA HS fluorometer assay and an Agilent TapeStation 2200 were used to assess RNA concentration post-extraction; initial Qubit values were 1890 ng/$\mu$L for~\syna.
The TapeStation was used throughout extraction and rRNA depletion to assess RNA quality and the extent of depletion, estimated from the absence of the 16S and 23S peaks (\sdqc).
Post poly-adenylation, DNA and buffer contamination were checked by spectroscopy (NanoDrop One) and the Qubit 1$\times$ dsDNA HS assay kit (ThermoFisher Scientific), and input RNA was finally quantified with the Qubit RNA HS assay kit before normalization to 9 $\mu$L containing 50--500 ng.

\subsubsection{ONT Library Preparation and Direct Sequencing}

Libraries were prepared with the ONT Direct RNA Sequencing Kit (SQK-RNA002) with slight modifications, using 0.5 $\mu$L of the RT adapter enzyme (reduced from 1 $\mu$L) and 4 $\mu$L of the RMX RNA adapter (reduced from 6 $\mu$L).
All samples were sequenced on R9.4 flow cells on the ONT GridION platform, with live base-calling enabled through the recommended MinKNOW (v23.07.5) scripts.
Base-calling used Guppy with the RNA trim strategy and calibration detection and filtering enabled: v6.1.3 (on NVIDIA A5000 GPUs) for the first~\syn~run and v7.0.9 for the second~\syn~run and the~\syna~run; called bases were 99.99--100\% confident in all run reports.
Two runs were generated for~\syn~and a single run for~\syna~(734.08~k reads, average N50 430).

\subsubsection{Mapping ONT Reads to the Reference Genomes}

Reads were retrieved from the NCBI SRA with the SRA Toolkit:~\syn~run 1 (SRR36199726, 234{,}477 reads),~\syn~run 2 (SRR36199725, 584{,}958 reads), and~\syna~(SRR36199724, 734~k reads).
Direct-RNA reads deposited in the native 3$'\rightarrow$5$'$ pore order (the~\syn~run 2 and the~\syna~run) were restored to 5$'\rightarrow$3$'$ sense orientation by reversing the base order (\texttt{seqkit} v2.1.0, reverse only and not reverse-complement, since direct-RNA reads are single-stranded), whereas the~\syn~run 1 reads were already in sense orientation.
Orientation was verified empirically: in the corrected orientation reads mapped sense to the annotated genes (over 97\% of gene-overlapping reads), whereas the opposite orientation mapped almost nothing.
Reads were aligned to the respective reference genome (\jsyn, NCBI accession CP002027.1;~\jsyna, CP016816.2) with \texttt{minimap2} v2.30~\cite{li_minimap2_2018} using the non-spliced long-read preset \texttt{-ax map-ont -p 0.99 --MD}, and sorted and indexed with \texttt{samtools} v1.22.1~\cite{li_sequence_2009}.
The \texttt{map-ont} preset was used in place of the splice-aware preset because~\mmy~and its reduced derivative are intron-less, so N-skip (spliced) alignments would bridge tens to hundreds of kilobases of unrelated reference.
Strandedness of the direct-RNA reads is preserved through the BAM flag bits, and per-strand sequencing-depth bedGraph tracks were computed with \texttt{samtools depth}, with forward-mapped reads defining the plus-strand transcripts.

For~\syn, run 1 yielded 207{,}480 primary mapped reads (88.5\%; mean length 661~nt, mean Phred quality Q17.5) and run 2 yielded 456{,}755 primary mapped reads (78.1\%; mean length 370~nt, Q31.7, 98.4\% above Q20), the higher quality of run 2 reflecting its newer base-caller.
For~\syna, 559~k of 734~k reads (76.2\%) mapped as primary alignments (mean length 383~nt, range 49--2{,}858~nt, mean quality Q31.2, 98.2\% above Q20); the 175~k unmapped reads (23.9\%) were of substantially lower quality (mean Q20.7, 61.0\% shorter than 300~nt), consistent with the expected tail of truncated ONT direct-RNA molecules rather than reference mismatch.
Secondary alignments accounted for 3--4\% of primary mapped reads in every run and clustered at the ribosomal RNA and tRNA loci of each genome (for~\syna, $\sim$55{,}460 and $\sim$343{,}267~bp, the two rRNA operons).

\subsubsection{Between-run Reproducibility and TPM Quantification}

Per-gene sense transcripts-per-million (TPM) were computed from the strand-specific depth tracks using the depth-based definition applied to the~\syn~Illumina and PacBio data.
The two~\syn~runs agreed only moderately (Pearson $r = 0.82$ on $\log_{10}$ TPM, with 36\% of genes differing more than two-fold), the largest discrepancies being residual rRNA transcripts, which the RiboMinus\textsuperscript{TM}-depleted run 1 retained far more abundantly than the NEBNext\textregistered{}-depleted run 2.
Because the two runs used different depletion chemistries and differ substantially, they were treated as independent runs rather than averaged into a single quantitative profile, and the pooled alignment was retained only as a genome-browser coverage track.
Each~\syn~run reproduced the~\syn~Illumina TPM profile with comparable fidelity (Pearson $r = 0.61$ and $0.60$ on $\log_{10}$ TPM for runs 1 and 2), confirming that the direct-RNA data recover the same transcriptome as the short-read and PacBio platforms.
The~\syna~ONT TPM profile was quantified in the same way and is reported alongside the~\syna~Illumina track (see the~\syna~short-read sequencing methods).
