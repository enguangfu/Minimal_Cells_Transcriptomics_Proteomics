\subsubsection{Isoform-based operon segmentation}

Of the 267~k clustered RNA isoforms, 4~k supported by $\geq$50 reads were retained to suppress noise from sequencing artifacts and RNase degradation fragments. These isoforms were grouped into candidate operons by containment clustering: two isoforms were placed in the same cluster if one was fully contained within the other (within a 10~bp boundary tolerance), implemented via a union-find algorithm with a coordinate sweep line. This produced 316 initial operons (148 on the plus strand, 168 on the minus strand; median length 2{,}100~bp), which were then annotated against the 911 canonical \textit{Syn1} genes, of which 307 operons contained at least one sense gene (108 single-gene, 90 two-gene, and 109 with three or more genes).

Same-strand operon overlaps (30 pairs, 26 of which shared at least one gene) were resolved in two steps. First, each overlapping pair was evaluated by a transcription-direction rule: if the downstream operon's TSS fell in an intergenic region or within 10~nt upstream of the nearest gene start (i.e.\ a plausible 5\textquotesingle\ UTR), the pair was kept separate as genuine independent promoters (15 pairs); otherwise they were merged as RNase processing artifacts (15 pairs), reducing the operon count from 316 to 301. Second, two additional same-strand operon pairs sharing identical sense gene loci were merged via a transitive union-find procedure, yielding 299 isoform-derived operons.

After the initial segmentation, 200 of 911 genes (22.0\%) remained uncovered by any isoform-derived operon. These were rescued through three complementary strategies: (i)~13 groups of consecutive uncovered genes (gap $\leq$500~bp) were recovered by identifying spanning isoforms in the BAM file, (ii)~the two rRNA operons (16S--23S--5S) were added manually, as their extreme secondary structure precludes faithful long-read capture, and (iii)~the remaining 166 uncovered genes with non-zero spanning reads were added as single-gene transcription units. The final operon map comprised 480 operons (283 from isoform clustering, 14 merged, 2 gene-combined, 15 multi-gene rescue, and 166 single-gene rescue) and achieved complete sense-strand coverage of all 911 annotated \textit{Syn1} genes (91.4\% sense-only, 5.8\% both sense and antisense, 2.7\% antisense-only, 0\% uncovered).
