The single starkest case of this reallocation is the $\sim$11~kb ribosomal-protein and translation-machinery operon \locus{0652}--\locus{0672}: this 21-gene polycistron, transcribed as one unit with no internal terminator, already supplied 12\% of the \syn~coding transcriptome and rose to 34\% in \syna, so that more than a third of all messenger RNA in the minimal cell originates from this one transcription unit (a $\sim$1.5-fold increase in relative output even after the reduced gene complement is accounted for); its primary transcript is further shaped by an internal endonucleolytic cleavage that leaves a highly abundant, stable 5$'$ fragment over the promoter-proximal genes.
